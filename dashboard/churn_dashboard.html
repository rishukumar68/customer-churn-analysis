Business Insights
01

Contract Type is the #1 Churn Predictor

Month-to-Month customers churn at 59.3% vs just 3.8–4.0% for annual/biennial contracts. Migrating even 10% of M2M customers to 1-year plans could save ~$180K/yr.

02

Fiber Optic — High Revenue, High Risk

Fiber customers pay the highest fees but churn at 54.8%. This segment accounts for the majority of revenue at risk. Quality and support issues must be addressed urgently.

03

Electronic Check = High Churn Signal

Customers paying by Electronic Check churn at 50.8% — more than double those using Bank Transfer (23.1%). Migrating to auto-pay reduces friction and improves retention.

04

Early Tenure is the Danger Zone

Churn peaks in the first 12 months (36–37%) then declines. Onboarding programs and proactive check-ins in months 1–6 are critical for long-term retention.

05

Tech Support Absence Doubles Churn

Customers without Tech Support churn significantly more. Bundling complimentary support trials with high-risk segments could reduce churn by an estimated 12–18%.

06

$1.68M Annual Revenue at Risk

The model identifies 1,836 high-risk active customers generating $140K/month. A targeted retention campaign costing even $200/customer would yield 7× ROI.

07

Satisfaction Score Inversely Tracks Churn

Customers rating satisfaction 1–2 churn at 60%+, while those rating 4–5 churn below 15%. Closing this gap through service improvements is the single highest-leverage action.

08

Support Calls Predict Dissatisfaction

Customers with 6+ support calls churn at nearly 2× the baseline rate. Proactive outreach after the 3rd support call could intercept churners before they decide to leave.
